## Decision recording rule

For each resolved decision add named decision maker, date, scope/target, selected value/procedure, evidence and consequences. Mark rejected alternatives only when needed to explain the choice. Do not infer approval from elapsed time, a passed local test or a historical hosting record. Approved D1–D9/P1–P5 and P4 lifetime retention remain the product baseline; this register does not reopen or replace them.

Next permissible proposal is Phase 6B disposable SQL Server/recovery/concurrency, isolated real browser/manual accessibility and explicitly scoped hosting verification.
